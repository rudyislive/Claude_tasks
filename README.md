# GTM Case Study Daily

A daily research routine that delivers **2 verifiable go-to-market (GTM) case studies** every morning — real companies that launched or scaled in roughly the last 3 years, each backed by clickable, checkable sources.

## Why this exists

The web is now flooded with AI-generated "case studies" that cite nothing and prove nothing. This routine does the opposite: **every claim is tied to a real source** — founder interviews, company growth/engineering blogs, podcasts (Lenny's, First Round Review, etc.), earnings, press, and primary data. Any number that can't be sourced is **flagged as unverified** rather than presented as fact.

## What each case study covers

- **Company snapshot** — founded, category, stage, funding/valuation, current scale
- **The GTM strategy** — the creative angle (best *and* boldest/failed attempts)
- **What they tried & what failed** — real experiments, dead ends, pivots
- **What worked / growth levers** — the mechanisms that actually compounded
- **A/B tests & experiments** — where disclosed
- **Channels** — how many, which ones, and whether it's growth hacking vs. growth marketing vs. traditional digital
- **Spend & ROI/ROAS** — where disclosed (flagged as undisclosed when companies don't share it)
- **Scaling approach** — how they went from PMF to scale
- **What you can steal** — the transferable takeaways
- **Sources** — numbered, clickable, with confidence notes

## How it runs

- **Schedule:** every day at **07:00 IST** (01:30 UTC)
- **Delivery:** committed to this repo under [`case-studies/`](case-studies/) **and** a Gmail draft to the owner
- **No repeats:** the routine reads [`case-studies/INDEX.md`](case-studies/INDEX.md) before choosing companies, so it never covers the same company twice
