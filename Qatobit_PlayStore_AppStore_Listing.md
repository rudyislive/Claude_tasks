## 6. Data Safety / Privacy Declaration

Privacy policy URL (**must be live before submission**): `https://qatobit.com/privacy`. Declare the following honestly; verify against actual SDKs — inaccurate declarations are the #1 cause of crypto-app rejections.

**Collected data — declare all:** Personal info · Financial info · Device or other IDs · App activity · App performance · Location.

| Data type | Detail | Shared? |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal info** | Name, email, phone (OTP/2FA), government ID (KYC). Encrypted in transit. User-deletable on account closure. | KYC vendor |
| **Financial info** | Transaction history, bank + UPI details, wallet balances, deposit/withdrawal records. Deletion subject to regulatory retention. | Payment/settlement partners |
| **Device IDs** | Android Advertising ID + Android ID. Mandatory per April 2025 Google Play policy. | Analytics SDKs |
| **App activity** | Interactions, screen views — Firebase / PostHog / Mixpanel active. | Analytics SDKs |
| **App performance** | Crash logs, diagnostics — Crashlytics or equivalent. | Analytics SDKs |
| **Location** | Approximate (IP-based jurisdiction checks). | — |

**Security:** data encrypted in transit; users can request deletion via `support@qatobit.com`. **Sharing:** data passed to third-party SDKs must be declared "Shared" under the April 2025 policy. **Do NOT declare:** health data, photos, contacts, calendar (not applicable).

Every major player hard-codes "Bitcoin" in the title and buries disclaimers at the end — **none lead with regulatory badges in the listing**, and none own "index / wealth."
